# Friday

Friday is a personal security assistant that runs on your own machine. It looks at the links you open and other activity on your computer, and tells you in plain language when something looks off and why.

It's open source and built with privacy in mind. Your browsing data stays on your computer unless you choose to turn on an online lookup.

> Friday is still early in development and isn't ready to use yet.

> **Please note:** no security tool can promise you're safe. Friday is meant to sit alongside your operating system's built-in protection and a good antivirus, not replace them.

## How it's meant to work

- **It stays local.** Nothing is sent anywhere unless you switch that feature on yourself.
- **Rules decide, AI explains.** Friday's checks are plain rules you can read. An optional local AI model can explain a result in everyday words, but it never makes the call.
- **You stay in control.** Friday won't delete, block, or change anything on your system without asking you first.
- **It's honest about uncertainty.** If Friday doesn't know, it says "unknown", never "safe".
